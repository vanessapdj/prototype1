Current operating principles
    Model boundaries & next audience

    All schools, geometry, reliability rates and support capacity are fictional. Default calendar has weekdays only; official school holidays and bookings are not loaded. Two day operators per school cover modeled robot-support tasks, not all school cleaning. Four toilet blocks are pilot coverage. Office jobs use the floor robots. Tank use, chemical dwell time, physical safety and continuous vendor supervision are outside this version. The engine exposes school-scoped data for a later supervisor interface; current controls are an HQ simulation, without user authentication.
